Lady Liberty

Lady Liberty was a gift proposed by french historian Edouard de Laboulaye. It was completed October 28, 1886. It was a gift meant to memorialise abolition of slavery and the preservation of democracy. It is truly a momunetal gift and one Americans hold close toheart. Its original colour was copper but over time it has oxodised into an eye catching green.

Central Park

Central Park is one of new yorks most beutiful comitments to nature. Though it is only the 5th biggest park in new york city it is a massive rectangular 843 acre plot that unapologetically dominates the part of the city it resides in. It is public to all filled with nature and open to numerous fun goverment and private events throughout the year.
