Intro to Florida

Florida is one of America's most coastal locations, with no point more than 60 miles from the sea. That means there's no shortage of fun water activity to enjoy! But that's not all, Florida is home to various other attractions with museums, landmarks and one of the most diverse ecosystems in the states. Whether you want to enjoy the warm waves, marvel at the wildlife or take in the scenery, Florida is the place for you!
