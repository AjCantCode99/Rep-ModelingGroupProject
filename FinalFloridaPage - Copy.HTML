Florida Travel Guide

Intro to Florida

Florida, also known as the 'Sunshine State' is one of America's most coastal locations, with no point more than 60 miles from the sea. That means there's no shortage of fun water activity to enjoy! But that's not all, Florida is home to various other attractions with museums, landmarks and one of the most diverse ecosystems in the states. Whether you want to enjoy the warm waves, marvel at the wildlife or take in the scenery, Florida is the place for you!

Main Attractions

Miami Beach

Miami Beach is actually its own municipality connected to mainland Miami by bridges. It holds the largest collection of Art Deco buildings, with over 800 across the city. There are several beaches scattered all over, with one for each of your wants. There are beaches with resorts, ones for nightlife and of course the classic quiet and relaxing locations.

Walt Disney World Resort

Better known as Disney World, this world famous theme park has something for everyone. Four theme parks, two water parks and 29,000 hotel rooms all themed after iconic Disney media all come together to make an unforgettable experience for anybody to enjoy. Each year the roster of characters and locations only grows, so now is the perfect time to visit!

Gatorland

Gatorland is a 110 acre theme and wildlife park situated in the south of Florida. It's known as the "Alligator Capital" of America due to the sheer amount of alligators and crocodiles you can find. This park is perfect for spotting some of Florida's natural inhabitants for any nature lovers. It has also been the backdrop for many films such as a 1984 Indiana Jones flick.
