USA Travel Guide

About the USA

The United States of America is one of the largest countries in the western world. With over 50 states there's no shortage of places to visit. The USA features over 63 national parks, all five major climate zones and major, world famous landmarks. So where to start? We have you covered. This travel guide features a comprehensive guide of 4 of the US's major states and how you can make the most of your visit.

The States we Feature

Florida

For any ocean lovers, Florida is the perfect place for you. With countless beaches of all shapes and sizes you can find no shortage of beautiful sands surrounded by crystal clear water. Florida also features tonnes of lush greenery supporting a huge population of wildlife. Florida also houses some of the most famous theme parks on the planet, meaning any thrill seekers would be at home here.

Texas

Spanning over 268,596 square miles, Texas is the second largest state in the USA. Home to vast deserts, rolling plains, and vibrant cities, the Lone Star State offers something for every traveller. Visitors can explore iconic landmarks, experience authentic cowboy culture, and enjoy some of the best barbecue in the United States. Texas is also rich in history and natural beauty, making it the perfect destination for anyone looking for a true taste of the American Southwest.

Hawaii

Hawaii is an ideal island getaway. Made up of stunning islands surrounded by crystal-clear waters, Hawaii offers breathtaking beaches, lush rainforests, and dramatic volcanic landscapes. Visitors can enjoy world-class surfing, snorkel among colourful marine life, or simply relax under swaying palm trees. With its unique culture, warm climate, and incredible natural beauty, Hawaii is the perfect destination for both adventure seekers and those looking to unwind.

New York

For those looking for excitement and opportunities to explore, New York is a fantastic destination. From towering skyscrapers to the scenic mountains and lakes found upstate, there is something for every traveller to enjoy. Visitors can experience world-famous landmarks, diverse cultures, and some of the best shopping, dining, and entertainment in the world. With its unique blend of urban energy and natural beauty, New York offers an unforgettable experience.
